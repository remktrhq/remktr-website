The four leaks

Your margin is capped by its weakest link. Find it. Fix it.

Most brand owners think they have a hundred ad problems. They usually have one leak, and it almost always sits in one of four places. See which one sounds like your account.

01

Defending what's already yours

Your brand terms and your top winners soak up budget every single week. Great for rank. But you can't tell which dollars are defending and which are actually winning new customers.

02

The expensive middle

Between your brand terms and your best sellers sits a broad middle of keywords you bid up every quarter. The same shoppers can be reached for a fraction per click. Nobody has ever rebuilt that middle.

03

Scaling that stalls

Every extra dollar buys a worse click. Turn spend up and ACoS climbs. Turn it down and rank slips. So the budget sits at the level you're already nervous about.

04

Nobody is really watching

Reports full of charts and no decisions. A junior hand-off after the pitch. Bids on autopilot between check-ins. Nobody owns the number that matters: what you pay to reach a shopper.

What is the free Growth Leak Audit?

A 30-minute screen-share on your own Amazon account. We show where your budget leaks at the top of the funnel, where buyers slip away after the click, put a number on what you're leaving on the table, and rank which of our four fixes pays back first.

What do I walk away with?

A written growth-leak teardown, your audience rebuild plan, a projected monthly recovery number, and 3–5 tactical next steps. They are yours whether or not we ever work together.

How do I book?

Fill out the short form on this page (about a minute). A calendar opens right after so you can pick your time. We prep your numbers before the call.

Do I have to change my keywords or listings?

No. Your proven winners and brand terms stay exactly where they are. We rebuild only the expensive middle of your traffic.

Is this right for my brand?

Size: if you are doing $1M+ a year on Amazon and spending $10K or more a month on Amazon ads, we can usually help. Under that, Dr. PPC is the better fit for now. What's the guarantee?

How does pricing work?

The audit itself is free. On the call we show you exactly how we are paid, before you commit to anything. No retainers. No creative fees.

What happens after the call?

If it is a fit, we walk you through how we would run it. If it is not, you keep the audit and the plan. No pressure either way.
