Background

Ken Freeman has more than 10 years of experience selling, building and running businesses on Amazon. He created one of the best-selling back braces on Amazon and was one of the earliest Amazon entrepreneurs to sell a company to Thrasio, a multiple-seven-figure exit.

He later served as VP of Amazon at a leading DTC brand for about three years, working on Amazon growth, advertising, marketplace strategy, brand protection and creative.

He founded Full Circle Agency: 100+ brands managed and $500M+ in Amazon revenue managed, with a team of more than 100 people in 10+ countries. Experienced operators own each account's strategy and execution, including Prime Day and Black Friday/Cyber Monday.

He has acquired multiple brands and launched brands of his own, and expanded the group by acquiring reMKTR, an Amazon DSP and performance-marketing company, which he rebuilt as a growth platform for established e-commerce brands.

He now builds Orbit, an AI-native operating system for Amazon commerce that pairs specialized AI agents with experienced operators, so smaller, more capable teams can run larger and more complex businesses.
