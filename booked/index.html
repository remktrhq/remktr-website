03Quick answers

Questions, answered.

Does this change my call with Jayce?

No. Your call is booked and stays exactly as it is. Dr. PPC is optional. Ask Jayce about it on the call and he'll show you how the two fit together.

What do I get for $1?

Dr. PPC plus the whole team (Ava, Bruno, Dr. Stock and Dr. Shield) and all of Orbit. Normally that's $300/month + 2% of ad spend. As a reMKTR client the $300 becomes $1 a month, permanently, and the 2% of ad spend stays.

Do I need to know PPC?

No. That's the point. Dr. PPC reads the account, builds the plan and explains his reasoning in plain language. You decide how much to approve.

Will he change my account without asking?

Only if you let him. At “Approve every move”, nothing reaches Amazon until you click approve. You can switch to “Inside guardrails” or “Fully autonomous”, and back, whenever you like.

Can you guarantee my ACoS or ROAS?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What we promise is process: every change has a reason you can read, a measurement window and a rollback trigger.

Is Dr. PPC a person?

No. Dr. PPC is software, powered by Opus 5.5 and built on the playbooks of an agency that has managed $500M+ in Amazon revenue.
