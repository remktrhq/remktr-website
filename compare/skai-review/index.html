Why Skai's pricing is described as opaque

Skai is unusual in this category because it publishes a price ladder at all. Pacvue's pricing URL returns a not-found page, and Tinuiti, Quartile, Criteo and Rithum quote on a call. So the perception of opacity is not that nothing is shown. It comes from how the published ladder works. Four reasons, each checkable on skai.io/pricing:

  • The fee is banded by annual media spend. Standard, Advanced, Enterprise and Enterprise Premier each carry a spend band. The price you pay depends on the media you forecast, not on a feature list, so two buyers with the same team can pay very different amounts.
  • The top is custom. Above the published bands there is a custom-quoted band. Large advertisers are the ones who most need a number, and they are the ones sent to a call.
  • Billing is annual. The published figures are annual, so the commitment is a year at a time. Divide by twelve yourself before you compare against a monthly tool.
  • Old pricing still circulates. Kenshoo became Skai in June 2021, and searches for Kenshoo pricing and Kenshoo Infinity reviews still land on material that describes an earlier product. A figure from one of those pages may not match today's ladder.

None of this is a criticism of Skai. Banded pricing is a reasonable way to price software whose cost to serve grows with spend. It just means the published number is where the conversation starts, not the whole answer.

What the published ladder tells you, and the arithmetic to run

As read on skai.io/pricing on 21 August 2026, the entry tier is $114,000 annually, for advertisers spending up to $4M a year in media. Check their page before you budget from it, because vendors revise these.

A banded annual fee means your effective rate changes with where you sit in the band. Divide the fee by your media spend:

  • At $1M of annual media, $114,000 is 11.4% of spend.
  • At $2M, it is 5.7%.
  • At the top of the band, $4M, it is 2.85%.

So the same tier is expensive for a buyer at the bottom of the band and cheap at the top. That is the single most useful fact for judging whether Skai is priced for you, and it is the reason a bare headline figure misleads in both directions. Compare it with a percentage-of-spend service, which charges the same rate at every level. We charge a percentage of media at reMKTR, so we are not telling you one model is better, only that the crossover is worth calculating with your own numbers.

Then ask for the rest of the bill in writing:

  • What happens in the month you cross a band threshold: a step up for the whole year, or a pro-rated change?
  • What is charged on top of the tier, such as onboarding, training, extra modules or support levels?
  • What does renewal look like, and what notice do you owe?
  • Is the price held for the term, and what applies if the company changes hands?

The objections buyers raise, turned into questions you can ask

Searches about Skai cluster around a handful of concerns: high cost, contractual limits, onboarding and implementation complexity, enterprise focus, support consistency, channel depth, data quality and ROI uncertainty. We cannot verify any of those as facts about Skai from a vendor page, so we will not repeat them as claims. Each is a fair diligence question for any platform, including ours. Put these to Skai and to everyone else on your shortlist:

  • Cost and contract: Ask for the term, auto-renewal, notice period and exit terms in writing before the demo ends.
  • Onboarding and implementation: Ask how many weeks until your first live campaign, who does the setup, and what is billed for it.
  • Enterprise focus: With a $114,000 entry tier, ask whether a team of your size gets the same support as the largest accounts.
  • Support: Date every review you read, then verify on a reference call with a customer of your size and channel mix.
  • Channel depth: Skai covers retail media, paid search and paid social. Ask for a demo of the one channel you spend most on, not the overview.
  • Data quality and ROI: Ask who designs and computes any incrementality test, and whether it is written into the contract.

The deeper question a platform review usually skips is who is going to drive it, for how many hours a week, and at what level of skill. A licence is a fixed cost that buys capability, not attention. The failure we see most often is a good platform bought by a small team with full calendars, run at a fraction of its capacity. Ask which person logs in daily, and what else is on their week. If the answer is a role rather than a name, the answer is nobody.

Disclosure, and what the rating covers

We are reMKTR. We buy Amazon DSP for brands on our own seats, which makes us an alternative to licensing a platform rather than a neutral reviewer of one. We would rather say that at the top than have you infer it later.

On G2, read 21 August 2026, Skai holds 4.0 out of 5 across 297 reviews. That is a large corpus for this category, where most platforms and agencies have fewer than fifty public reviews. A score without its count is not a fact, which is why both appear here.

One caveat, and it is not a criticism. Kenshoo became Skai in June 2021, and the company's footer still reads "Kenshoo, Ltd". A corpus built across that transition describes at least two different products. A practical method:

  • Filter to the last twelve months first. Older reviews describe the platform before its current AI features and, further back, before the rename.
  • Filter to your channel mix. A five-star review from a search-heavy advertiser says little about the retail media module.
  • Read the three-star reviews. They tend to describe fit honestly.
  • Then verify on a reference call. Support and onboarding change fastest and date worst.

This applies equally to Pacvue, Intentwise, DataHawk and Rithum, which have all repositioned. One more neutral point: Skai was reported to have held M&A discussions in 2024 that did not conclude. Nothing follows about the product, but change-of-control, price-protection and data-portability clauses are worth reading before any multi-year licence, from any vendor.

What Skai is genuinely good at

Breadth in one console. Skai positions as an AI-powered commerce media platform and states integration with 300+ publishers, across retail media, paid search and paid social. If your team runs Amazon alongside Walmart, Instagram and Google and reconciles four exports in a spreadsheet every Monday, that consolidation is the product, and it is a real job being done.

A published price ladder in a category that mostly quotes on a call. Named tiers with dollar figures and spend bands let you model total cost before a single call. Printing a ladder changes where your negotiation starts. On transparency, Skai is ahead of us: we publish a price for Dr. PPC, but not for reMKTR.

Amazon DSP experience. Skai's own case studies cite Amazon DSP results, including a 108% increase in Amazon DSP revenue and an 80% lift in new-to-brand return. Those are the vendor's figures about its customers, so treat them the way you would treat ours.

What to do with this

Judge this on the job you need done, not the feature list. Ask how they would prove display added sales: a holdout set before launch, and DSP and sponsored ads reconciled in Amazon Marketing Cloud. A partner who can only show last-click ROAS is showing you sales your search ads may have won anyway.

Common questions

Why is Skai's pricing seen as opaque?

Because the published ladder is only the start. Annual tiers are banded by media spend, the top band is custom-quoted, billing is annual, and older Kenshoo-era pricing still circulates. Ask what is charged on top of the tier and what happens when you cross a spend threshold.

Does Skai publish its pricing?

Yes, which is unusual here. skai.io/pricing lists named tiers with dollar figures on annual billing, each tied to a band of annual media spend, plus a custom band above. As read on 21 August 2026, the entry tier is $114,000 up to $4M of media. Confirm against their current page before you budget.

Is Skai the same company as Kenshoo?

Yes. Kenshoo rebranded to Skai in June 2021, and the company's footer still reads "Kenshoo, Ltd". That is why the old name persists in search results, and why some reviews and pricing pages describe an earlier product.

What is Skai's rating?

G2 shows 4.0 out of 5 across 297 reviews, read 21 August 2026. The corpus spans the rename and a product repositioning, so filter to the last twelve months before weighting it.

Should I license a platform or hire an agency for Amazon DSP?

Name the person who will log in daily and say what else is on their week. If that person exists and has capacity, a licence can be better economics. If nobody has the hours, a licence is a fixed cost driven at a fraction of capacity, and buying the buying may be the more efficient purchase.
