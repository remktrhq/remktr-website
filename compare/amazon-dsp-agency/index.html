The short answer

An Amazon DSP agency buys programmatic display, video and audio advertising on Amazon's demand-side platform. This is advertising, not delivery driving — Amazon DSP also stands for Delivery Service Partner, the courier franchise, and that is an entirely different business. Agencies hold DSP seats and run campaigns for brands.

Two things share this acronym, and only one is on this page

Amazon uses DSP for two unrelated businesses, and the search results mix them together constantly.

  • Demand-Side Platform — the programmatic advertising product. Brands and agencies use it to buy display, video and audio placements on Amazon's own properties and across third-party sites and apps. That is what this page is about.
  • Delivery Service Partner — the courier franchise programme, where independent owners run delivery vans, routes, drivers and dispatch for Amazon packages.

If you arrived looking for delivery work or the franchise programme, you want logistics.amazon.com, not an advertising agency. No hard feelings — the collision is Amazon's, not yours.

Everything below concerns advertising: media buying, audiences, creative and measurement.

What the job actually involves

A DSP agency is not a reporting service. The weekly work is concrete:

  • Audience construction. Building and maintaining segments from Amazon shopping behaviour — remarketing, in-market, lifestyle, competitor conquesting, plus your own first-party lists where they exist.
  • Inventory and placement decisions. Where the impressions run, on and off Amazon, and what gets excluded.
  • Creative production and rotation. Display and video units in the right sizes, refreshed before fatigue sets in. This is real production work, not a checkbox.
  • Budget pacing and bid strategy against a target that reflects your actual margins rather than a category benchmark.
  • Measurement. Reconciling DSP against sponsored ads so the two are not both claiming the same order, and testing whether display caused sales rather than observed them.

That last item is where most engagements are won or lost, and it is the one least often demonstrated in a pitch.

Seats, minimums, and the three ways to buy

Access to Amazon DSP is not open self-signup for everyone, which is why agencies exist in this category at all.

  • Amazon's managed service. Amazon runs the campaigns. Their own product page states this option typically requires a minimum investment of USD 50,000, and notes that it varies by country. Suits advertisers with limited programmatic experience who want Amazon's own team.
  • Self-service. You hold the seat and control everything after account creation. This needs a trader on staff who lives in the platform, not a marketing generalist with a spare afternoon.
  • Through an agency holding seats. Amazon DSP is available to brand advertisers, advertising agencies and tool providers. Agency minimums vary and are generally more flexible than the direct managed route — some start in the low five figures a month. Ask each agency for theirs in writing.

Fee structures are usually a percentage of media spend, sometimes with a base. reMKTR charges a percentage of media spend and states it before you sign; what matters is that you know the number, what it applies to, and where it steps down.

Five questions that separate agencies

Every agency will show you a case study with a large multiple on it. These questions get past that.

  • "Show me a portfolio number, not a best campaign." Ask for the aggregate across the book, with the advertiser count and the month named. A ROAS without a denominator is not evidence.
  • "How do you separate DSP from sponsored ads?" If both channels report the same purchases, your blended return is inflated and nobody has noticed. The reconciliation happens in Amazon Marketing Cloud or it does not happen.
  • "When did you last run a holdout, and what did it show?" A partner who has never withheld display from a matched group has never tested whether it works.
  • "Who is on my account weekly, and how many others do they hold?" The pitch team is rarely the delivery team.
  • "What is in the fee?" Creative production, Amazon Marketing Cloud work and audience development are sometimes inside and sometimes billed on top.

When you should not hire a DSP agency

Three situations where the answer is no, and we would rather tell you now than four months in.

Your sponsored ads are leaking. Display amplifies whatever your account already does. If a large share of search spend is going to terms that produce no orders, fix that first — the same budget returns more there, and display gets more efficient afterwards because the retargeting pool is cleaner. Your inventory cannot support the demand. Driving display traffic to items that go out of stock mid-flight wastes the spend and damages rank. You have a capable in-house trader. Then buy the seat, not the service. You will pay less and move faster, and a managed engagement will feel like a layer between you and your own account.

Which one you should actually pick

Hire an Amazon DSP agency when Amazon is a serious share of revenue, sponsored ads are already tidy, inventory is stable, and nobody in-house can run programmatic weekly. Go direct to Amazon or self-service if you have a trader and the spend to clear their minimum. Fix search or inventory first if either is leaking.

What to do with this

Shortlist on the job, not the feature grid. Pull your search-term report for the last 90 days and total the spend against terms that produced no orders — 33.6% on the account above. Then ask each vendor on your list what they would do about it in week one, and see who answers with a process rather than a screenshot.

Common questions

Is Amazon DSP the same as being an Amazon delivery partner?

No. Amazon Demand-Side Platform is programmatic advertising. Amazon Delivery Service Partner is the courier franchise programme with vans, drivers and routes. They share three letters and nothing else. For the delivery programme, go to logistics.amazon.com.

What is the minimum spend for Amazon DSP?

Amazon's own product page describes a typical minimum investment of USD 50,000 for its managed-service option, varying by country. Agencies holding their own seats generally set lower minimums, sometimes in the low five figures monthly. Ask any agency for its minimum in writing before you plan a budget.

How much does an Amazon DSP agency cost?

Most charge a percentage of media spend, sometimes with a base fee. The percentage is what matters: at $100,000 a month in spend, one point is $12,000 a year. Ask for the rate, what it applies to, whether creative is included, and where it steps down.

Do I need sponsored ads running before starting DSP?

Usually yes. Display works best against a healthy account with clean retargeting pools and stable inventory. Starting display on top of a leaking search account tends to buy expensive traffic to a page that was already converting badly.

How do I know DSP is actually working?

Withhold it from a matched group and compare. Attribution reports tell you which orders touched an ad; a holdout tells you which orders would not have happened otherwise. Reconcile the two channels in Amazon Marketing Cloud so DSP and sponsored ads are not both claiming the same purchase.
