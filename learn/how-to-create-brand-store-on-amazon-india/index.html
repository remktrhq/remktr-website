The steps in order, and what each one actually requires

There are five things that have to happen, and skipping the order is why most first attempts stall. You cannot open Store Builder without Brand Registry approval, and you cannot get Brand Registry approval without a trademark that Amazon's India team can verify.

  • Enroll in Brand Registry with a trademark registered in India, or a pending one filed through IP Accelerator.
  • Confirm your seller account is on a professional selling plan on Amazon.in with an active GSTIN — this is a general seller-account requirement, not something specific to Stores, but it will block you if it's missing.
  • Open Store Builder from Seller Central or the advertising console once Brand Registry shows approved.
  • Build your pages using templates or a blank layout, dragging in tiles for images, video, and product grids.
  • Submit for review and fix anything Amazon flags before it goes live.

The part almost every guide glosses over is that steps one and two are India-specific, not global defaults, and that's where the real friction sits.

Brand Registry in India: the trademark question everyone stalls on

Amazon India will not accept a trademark registered only in the US, EU, or anywhere else. It has to be filed with the Indian Trade Marks Registry, and the mark class has to cover the products you're actually selling. A skincare brand registered under a class that doesn't include cosmetics will get bounced even if the name matches perfectly.

If you don't have a registered mark yet, Amazon's IP Accelerator connects you with an empanelled Indian law firm to file one, and grants provisional Brand Registry access once the application is filed — not once it's granted. That's the detail people miss: you don't have to wait years for full registration to start building your store, but you do have to actually file, not just intend to.

The brand name you enter in Store Builder has to match the name on the trademark filing exactly, down to spacing and capitalization conventions Amazon's system checks against. A mismatch here is one of the most common rejection reasons, and it's entirely avoidable if you check it before submitting anything.

Building the store in Store Builder

Once Brand Registry shows approved, Store Builder is the same drag-and-drop tool across every Amazon marketplace, including India. You choose a template or start blank, then add tiles: hero images, product grids, video, shoppable galleries, and text blocks for your brand story.

Single page or multi-page depends on catalog size, not preference. A brand with under ten SKUs usually doesn't need more than a homepage and a products tab — extra pages just dilute the traffic each page gets. A brand selling across several distinct product lines benefits from a multi-page structure with its own tab per category, because it lets a shopper who came in for one product browse into an adjacent one without leaving the store.

Preview every page in mobile view before you submit. Most Amazon India traffic is mobile, and a tile layout that looks fine on desktop can crowd or crop badly on a phone screen — Amazon's review doesn't catch this for you, only broken links and policy violations do.

When Amazon rejects your submission

Rejections are common on a first submission and almost never fatal. The usual causes: a tile linking to a page outside Amazon, a headline making a price or promotional claim (Store content policy prohibits both), or a brand name that doesn't match the trademark on file character for character.

We've had a submission stall because a tile linked to a listing that had gone out of stock between building the page and submitting it — Amazon's review checks whether a link resolves, not whether the product behind it still makes sense to feature. Click through every tile yourself right before you submit, not just when you built it.

If a submission comes back rejected, fix the specific flagged item and resubmit — you don't need to rebuild the whole store, and there's no penalty for resubmitting more than once.

A store without traffic is a business card no one opens

Building the store is the easy half. Amazon's own published numbers show shoppers who visit a Store during their shopping journey buy more often and place higher-value orders than shoppers who don't — but that only matters if shoppers actually land on the page. A Store with no inbound traffic sits at zero regardless of how good the layout is.

The two levers that send traffic there are Sponsored Brands, which can point directly to a Store instead of a single listing, and DSP display, which can point to a Store from off-Amazon placements. Across 30 of our advertisers in July 2026, the book delivered 6.04x return on ad spend — measured across the whole book, not cherry-picked from the best line item — and a chunk of that came from traffic landing on Store pages built to hold a browsing shopper rather than a single product detail page.

Catalog breadth changes what the store should even try to do. A brand with one hero product needs a Store built to close a sale. A brand with a wide catalog — grooming lines like Beardbrand, gardening ranges like Epic Gardening — needs a Store built to cross-sell, because the page's job shifts from converting one visit to extending it.

Common questions

Is it free to create a Brand Store on Amazon India?

Yes. Creating and maintaining a Store costs nothing beyond your existing professional seller account. If you outsource design or content production, that's a separate cost — Amazon's tool itself is free to use.

Can I open a Brand Store in India without a registered trademark?

Only through IP Accelerator, which pairs you with an approved Indian law firm and grants provisional Brand Registry access once the trademark application is filed, not once it's fully granted. Without that route, you need a mark already registered with the Indian Trade Marks Registry.

Does a Brand Store improve organic search rank on Amazon India?

Amazon doesn't publish organic rank as a direct benefit of owning a Store. What it does confirm is a shopping-behavior lift — higher add-to-cart rate and order value among shoppers who visit a Store — and that behavior lift can indirectly support ranking signals over time, which is a different mechanism than the SEO boost some guides imply.

How long does Amazon take to approve a Store submission in India?

Amazon doesn't publish a fixed review window for Store submissions, so build slack into your launch timeline rather than assuming a set number of days. Check status inside Store Builder instead of guessing.

What's the most common reason Store submissions get bounced back in India?

Name mismatch between the trademark on file and the brand name entered in Store Builder, and tiles linking to external URLs or making price claims — both violate Amazon's Store content policy and trigger an automatic rejection.
