  • The Amazon services niche is a classic low-volume, extreme-CPC B2B market: head terms of 90–1,000 searches per month carry CPCs of $45–$140+. Source: reMKTR keyword-universe analysis of 760 classified keywords, Google + Bing, August 2026.
  • A single "best amazon ads agency" click at the $398 top-of-page bid costs more than many brands' entire daily sponsored-ads budget on Amazon itself. Source: comparison of Google Ads bid data (August 2026) with reMKTR portfolio budget norms.
  • Software-brand terms like "pacvue demo" ($192) and "perpetua pricing" ($134.78) reveal how much a demo-ready enterprise buyer is worth to Amazon-tool vendors. Source: Google Ads keyword-planning data, US, August 2026.
  • The cheapest high-intent clicks in the whole ecosystem remain on Amazon DSP itself: $0.21–$0.44 in our campaigns — under 1% of what the services market pays for a Google click. Source: reMKTR DSP campaign data, 2026. Full context on our Amazon DSP Statistics page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average CPC on Amazon in 2026?

There is no single average — cost per click is category-driven. In our managed accounts, sponsored-ads CPCs range from under $1 in low-competition niches to $8+ to break into competitive supplement categories, with category averages of $5.00–$6.60 where we run DSP comparisons. Amazon DSP display and online-video CPCs in our campaigns run $0.21–$0.44.

Why are Amazon DSP CPCs so much lower than Sponsored Products CPCs?

Sponsored ads are keyword auctions at the moment of purchase intent — every competitor bids on the same handful of high-intent terms. DSP is an audience auction across Amazon's owned and third-party inventory, where far fewer advertisers compete. That's how our DSP campaigns buy clicks at $0.21–$0.44 while the same categories' sponsored auctions clear at $5.00–$6.60.

How much does it cost to advertise an Amazon agency or software on Google?

From Google keyword-planning data pulled in August 2026 (US): "amazon ad agency" averages $141.38 per click, "best amazon ads agency" shows a $398 top-of-page bid, "amazon advertising agency" $114.60, "amazon dsp agency" $63.90, "pacvue demo" $192, and "perpetua pricing" $134.78. The Amazon services market is one of the most expensive B2B niches on Google.

How much do Amazon CPCs rise during Prime Day?

Across our managed portfolio, sponsored-ads CPCs rose +116% during Prime week 2026. Traffic and conversion rise too, so the event still pays for prepared brands — but budgets should be planned around roughly double the normal click cost.
