Straight answers.

How do Amazon brands scale past PPC? +

By adding the shoppers search doesn't reach: people who viewed and left, people in your category who never searched your keyword, and people watching streaming TV. Amazon's audience network reaches all three with Amazon's own shopping signals.

Will this work for my category? +

We've run it for supplements, pet care, beauty, kitchen, games, home and outdoor brands. The free audit shows what fits your brand before you commit to anything.

Who is it for? +

Amazon brands spending $10K+ a month on Amazon ads. Thirty-minute audit, and if we're a fit, live in as little as 1 week.

What is reMKTR? +

reMKTR (say it "re-market-er") is an ad-tech company for Amazon brands. We combine Amazon's audience network, our Target Lock AI and commercial-grade video production, and run it all for you, so you can scale beyond traditional Amazon PPC. We're a Full Circle company.

How is this different from my sponsored ads? +

Sponsored ads catch shoppers at the moment they search. That's valuable, and it's also where everyone is bidding. reMKTR goes after everyone else: people who viewed and left, people in your category who never typed your keyword, and people watching streaming TV.

Is the first 30 days really free? +

Yes. Your first 30 days with reMKTR are free. We'll walk you through exactly how it works on the call, before you commit to anything.

What's the guarantee? +

Your overall ROAS improves, or we work for free until it does. We agree the starting baseline with you up front, so we both measure the same number.

How do you charge after the free month? +

A percentage of ad spend. No retainers. No creative fees. We'll give you the exact percentage on the call.
